SymWrite / a writing experiment

What happens between a thought and its next sentence?

I’m interested in what it would mean for a writing assistant to be aligned with a particular person. A model can learn the texture of someone’s prose and still miss what they are trying to say. The interesting problem is how to give it useful context while leaving the direction of the thought open to the writer.

SymWrite is a small experiment in that direction. It brings together memory, several possible continuations, and a second pass at synthesis. Each of these stages makes a choice. I want the writer to be able to see enough of those choices to disagree with them.

01

Choose what to remember

When you request a continuation, SymWrite searches the writing samples and approved facts in the selected profile. It retrieves up to four relevant excerpts, then combines them with style guidance and the text around your cursor. This is retrieval and prompting; the app does not train a new model on your writing.

02

Explore, then synthesize

The candidate model tries up to five directions in parallel: an implication, a concrete mechanism, an assumption, a connection, and a more economical continuation. In Refine mode, a second model draws on the candidates and the same source context to propose one continuation. Explore stops before that second pass.

03

Return the choice to the writer

You can compare the candidates with the refinement and inspect the excerpts under Sources. Nothing enters the draft until you accept it. You can edit the result, undo the insertion, or dismiss the suggestions and keep writing. I want the alternatives to remain available because synthesis can discard something that mattered.

Where the writing goes

Drafts are saved in this browser. A continuation request sends the draft to the app server; only bounded text around the cursor, style guidance, and retrieved excerpts go to the model provider. The profile’s retrieval index and provider credentials stay on the server. The current example uses Qwen for candidates and DeepSeek for refinement through OpenRouter.

What I’m still trying to understand

Five continuations can repeat the same assumption. A refinement can quietly remove a qualification. I’d like to test whether seeing the alternatives and their context helps a writer notice those changes. The pipeline makes this question easier to examine; it does not establish that the model understands the person using it.
